# PRICE-2291 — Doepfer line: where do we actually sit?

**Raised by** Katrin (Sales) · **For** Pricing · **Priority** High

## Why I'm asking

We lost the Hamburg studio account last month, and the buyer told me straight out
that he could get the same Doepfer modules from the States for less, delivered. I had
nothing to argue back with. Every time anyone asks what the Americans charge we end up
guessing from one or two product pages somebody screenshotted months ago.

Doepfer is our steadiest line — it is half the reason studios call us at all. If we
are genuinely uncompetitive on it, I need to know before the next quarter's buy, not
after we lose two more accounts.

## What I need

Their Doepfer range with prices, in something the pricing team can put **next to our
own list** — not a screenshot, not a PDF. Something they can actually join to our
catalogue, module by module.
