# PRICE-0912 — Bambu Lab accessories: procurement wants numbers

**Raised by** Sanne (Sales) · **For** Pricing · **Priority** Medium

## Why I'm asking

Procurement is placing the next Bambu Lab order in two weeks and has asked, reasonably,
whether our margin on the accessory line is defensible. Our print-farm customers buy
these as consumables — nozzles, plates, hotend spares — so they reorder constantly and
they notice price before they notice anything else.

Two US specialists stock essentially the same accessory range. If they are materially
under us, procurement wants to know before committing.

## What I need

Both catalogues with prices, matched against ours so the pricing team can work item by
item rather than on a blended average. Blended averages are how we got into this.

## The two specialists

| | Shop | Their range |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | West3D | https://west3d.com/collections/bambu-bambu-lab-printer-accessories (~187 products) |
| **B** | Fabreeko | https://fabreeko.com/collections/bambu-labs (~102 products) |

Note these two are not the same size, so I would like the overlap stated plainly — how
many products genuinely appear in both, not just the headline counts.
